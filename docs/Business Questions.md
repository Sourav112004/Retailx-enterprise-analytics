#  RetailX Business Questions

## Executive Overview

### Business Performance

1. What is the company's total revenue?
2. How many orders have been successfully completed?
3. How many unique customers have purchased from the platform?
4. What is the average order value (AOV)?
5. What percentage of deliveries are completed late?
6. What percentage of customer reviews are negative?
7. How has monthly revenue changed throughout the year?
8. Which states generate the highest revenue for the business?

---

## Sales & Product Analytics

### Product Performance

1. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
2. Which product categories contribute the largest share of total company revenue?
3. Which product categories have the highest repeat purchase rate?
4. Which product categories should receive more marketing and inventory investment?
5. Which categories underperform despite having high sales volume?

### Seller Performance

6. Which sellers generate the highest revenue?
7. Which sellers consistently outperform the rest of the marketplace?
8. How concentrated is revenue among the top-performing sellers?
9. Which sellers should be rewarded or used as performance benchmarks?

## Customer Insights

### Customer Value

1. What is the average Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)?
2. How much revenue has each customer generated over their lifetime?
3. What is the average order value per customer?
4. How many orders does an average customer place?

### Customer Retention

5. How many customers return and purchase again?
6. What is the monthly customer retention rate?
7. How many customers are retained month over month?
8. Are customers becoming more loyal over time?
9. Which customers contribute the greatest long-term business value?

# Strategic Business Questions Answered

Beyond reporting KPIs, this project answers several strategic questions:

• Is business growth driven by new customers or repeat customers?

• Is company revenue dependent on a small number of product categories?

• Which customers should be prioritized for retention?

• Which sellers contribute the most to marketplace success?

• Which geographical regions require logistics optimization?

• Which operational metrics should executives monitor daily?

• Where should management invest to maximize revenue growth?

• Which business areas create the greatest opportunity for improving customer satisfaction?

---

# Business Impact

The RetailX analytics solution enables stakeholders to:

• Monitor company-wide KPIs from a single executive dashboard.

• Identify high-performing products and sellers.

• Measure customer lifetime value and retention.

• Detect logistics bottlenecks before they impact customer experience.

• Support strategic decision-making using SQL-driven business insights instead of raw transactional data.
